About Guidee

Some visionaries change the world in real life. Nikola Tesla turned imagination into inventions that still power our world.

Some visionaries live in stories. Tony Stark is fictional, but he embodies the daring mix of creativity, intelligence, and courage that inspires millions.

Guidee is built for learners who carry both sparks within them—the Tesla who builds, and the Stark who dreams.

The Two Sigma Problem

Research shows that students with personal, one-on-one tutoring perform two standard deviations better than their peers in traditional classrooms. That’s the Two Sigma Problem: the average tutored student outperforms 98% of classroom learners.

The challenge has always been that personal tutoring is expensive, rare, and nearly impossible to scale.

Guidee is designed to solve this. We make the power of personal guidance accessible to anyone, anywhere.

The Socratic Method

Real learning doesn’t come from being told answers. It comes from asking questions, testing ideas, and discovering understanding for yourself. Our entire platform is built on the Socratic methodology:

Ask a question → Explore possibilities → Break down the reasoning → Arrive at clarity.

This method doesn’t just teach facts. It teaches how to think, question, and create, the way great innovators always have.
