Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 3, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information.

What Data We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address, Google User ID, and the username you choose.
  • User-Generated Content: We store the content you create, including guidelets, lessons, and reviews.
  • Usage Data: We track your enrollments, progress (including chat history and completed chapters), and XP earned to provide the core service.
  • Technical Data: Our hosting provider (Render) may collect technical data such as your IP address and browser type for logging and security purposes.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain our service.
  • To personalize your experience on the platform.
  • To improve our service by analyzing feature usage.

Third-Party Services (Data Sub-processors)

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google: For authentication.
  • Render: For hosting our application and database.
  • Cloudinary: For storing media files (images and audio).
  • Google (Gemini): For processing lesson scripts and chat messages to generate AI responses.

Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access your data at any time.
  • Correct your data via the "Edit Profile" page.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.

Cookies

We use a necessary cookie for session management to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at jeevan@guidee.me
